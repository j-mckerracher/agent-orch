This was outside the pinned source checkout and was not one of the 34 P12r3 gates. The primary checkout's tracked status after the incident was unchanged (`main` behind its remote by one commit and the pre-existing untracked watcher utility); `git diff --stat` was empty. `apps/desktop/node_modules` exists now; its prior state is unknown, so no rollback or replacement was attempted. The isolated P12r3 `desktop-npm-ci` and `desktop-package-win` gates ran separately in the pinned source checkout and passed. The earlier P12r3 evidence commits are preserved; this note does not rewrite them.
